You are the engineer. Your life is the system.
MYTRA is the telemetry.

Instrumentation, not judgement

MYTRA reports against your baseline — not a population average, not an arbitrary target. Every number is a reading, not a verdict.

Mission control

Energy, focus, sleep and routine completion on one telemetry panel, with a 24-hour timeline of everything you logged.

Focus engine

Free-running sessions or Pomodoro cycles — 25/5 and 45/15 — that switch phase automatically and chime when a phase closes. Every question afterwards is optional.

Sleep & recovery

Duration, bedtime consistency and nap logging, tracked against how the following day actually went.

Caffeine ledger

Per-teaspoon dose calculation for ground and instant coffee, plus espresso, tea and custom entries. Time-since readout on the dashboard.

Intelligence engine

Correlations computed from your own history — sleep against focus, caffeine timing against sleep, naps against energy — each with a sample size and a confidence tier.

Weekly system report

Strongest variable, degraded variable, best and lowest day, and the single most interesting signal in the last seven days.

Signals, not slogans

Every observation MYTRA surfaces carries three things: what the data shows, how much data supports it, and an experiment you could run to test it.

Example signal
Moderate signal

Focus has averaged higher on days following longer sleep.

8.1 / 10 after nights of 7h 30m or more, against 6.9 / 10 after shorter nights.

Evidence18 comparable days in your history.
ExperimentProtect a 7h 30m sleep opportunity for 7 days and see whether the pattern holds.

Correlation is not causation, and MYTRA never claims otherwise. It shows you where to look.

Your data belongs to you

Everything you log is stored against your account and nothing else. Export it as JSON at any time, or delete all of it in one action. No advertising, no data sale, no third-party analytics.

Not medical software

MYTRA supports self-reflection and behaviour change. It does not diagnose, treat or manage any medical or mental-health condition, and it can make mistakes. It is not a substitute for a doctor, therapist, psychologist, sleep specialist or emergency service. If you are concerned about your health, consult a qualified physician.
